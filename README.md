# 📊 Business Performance & Profitability Dashboard (Excel)

![Business Performance Dashboard](business_performance_dashboard.png)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Project Overview
An interactive executive dashboard designed and engineered in **Microsoft Excel** to monitor business profitability, product category margins, and territorial expansion across **$2.29 Million in total revenue**. The model incorporates dynamic **Slicers (Region, Customer Segment, Product Category)**, multi-year sales trends (2016–2019), and granular subcategory profitability auditing.
---

## 🎯 Global Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** $2,297K ($2.29M)
* **Total Operating Profits:** $286K
* **Overall Profit Margin:** ~12.45%
* **Top Performing Region by Revenue:** West ($725K)
* **Primary Customer Segment:** Consumer (51% of Total Volume)

  ---

## 🔍 In-Depth Analytical Breakdown

### 1. Subcategory Profitability & Deficit Discovery
* **Top Profit Engines:** **Copiers ($56K)**, **Phones ($45K)**, and **Accessories ($42K)** yield the highest operating returns in the Technology line.
* **Loss Leader Alert:** The **Tables** subcategory generated a significant net operating loss of **-$18K**, highlighting severe pricing or discount inefficiency within Furniture.
* **Steady Office Supplies:** **Paper ($34K)** and **Binders ($30K)** maintain reliable margins with minimal capital risk.

### 2. Regional Footprint & Segment Margins
* **Revenue Concentration:** **West ($725K)** and **East ($678K)** represent the primary revenue drivers, followed by **Central ($501K)** and **South ($392K)**.
* **Segment Profitability by Region:**
  * **Consumer Segment:** Delivers the highest absolute profit in the **West ($57K)** and **East ($41K)**.
  * **Central Region Vulnerability:** Central shows compressed profitability across Consumer ($9K) and Home Office ($12K).

### 3. Customer Segmentation Breakdown
* **Consumer:** Dominates aggregate sales volume at **51%**.
* **Corporate:** Represents **31%** of aggregate volume.
* **Home Office:** Accounts for **19%** of volume with a steady year-over-year upward trajectory.

### 4. Seasonality & Multi-Year Growth (2016 – 2019)
* **Intra-Year Seasonality:** Notable revenue dips occur in **February**, with explosive seasonal demand surges in **September** and **November**.
* **Multi-Year Momentum:** All three customer segments demonstrate strong secular growth from 2016 through 2019, with the Consumer line showing the steepest acceleration curve.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation in Excel
* **Pivot Tables & Calculated Fields:** Built aggregated summary tables powering multi-variable chart visualizations.
* **Cross-Filtering Slicers:** Implemented interconnected slicers for **Region** (Central, East, South, West), **Segment** (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office), and **Category** (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).
* **Visual Formatting & Chart Selection:** Applied tailored horizontal bar charts for deficit/margin visualization, 100% stacked profit distribution columns, and multi-line time series curves.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Remediate Tables Subcategory:** Audit discounting rules and freight costs on Tables to reverse the -$18K margin erosion, or shift floor space toward high-margin Copiers and Phones.
2. **Central Region Margin Restructuring:** Investigate supply chain and distribution overheads in the Central territory to elevate margin parity with the West.
3. **Q4 Demand Planning:** Scale inventory ahead of the September–November peak to eliminate stockouts during high-velocity purchasing windows.
